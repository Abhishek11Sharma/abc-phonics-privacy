Privacy Policy

ABC Phonic Adventure · Android app · Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: ABC Phonic Adventure is a phonics game for young children. It collects no personal data and makes no network requests.

Information we collect

None. ABC Phonic Adventure has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Progress, stars and settings are saved only on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Read-aloud (text-to-speech)

Letters and words are spoken using the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. The app does not record audio or use the microphone.

Permissions

  • The Android app framework declares the standard internet permission, but ABC Phonic Adventure itself makes no network requests and sends no data anywhere.

Children's privacy

ABC Phonic Adventure is made with children in mind. It does not collect, use or disclose personal information from anyone, including children, in line with Google Play's Families policy and COPPA.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
